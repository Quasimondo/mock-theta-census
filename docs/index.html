mock-theta-census

A computational search for Ramanujan-style mock theta functions. A growth test at 19 roots of unity classifies q-series; a census of 8,869 functions finds 25 mock theta functions, all identified; and two of them lead to the sign-twisted fifth-order companions ψ₁*, ψ₀*.

ExplorerMock theta functions on the unit disk: watch f(q) blow up at the roots of unity, and compare ψ₁ with the new ψ₁*, ψ₀*. FingerprintsThe classifier's results for 51 known functions, the census, and what ψ₁*, ψ₀* turned out to be. Formula sheetEvery formula in LaTeX, Unicode, plain text and Mathematica, with coefficient lists. Code and dataThe census scripts, results, and the write-ups on GitHub.
